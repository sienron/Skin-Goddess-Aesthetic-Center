Objectives  
General  objectives  
The  general  objective  of  this  study  is  to  design  and  develop  a  Web-Based  Appointment  
and
 
Inventory
 
Management
 
System
 
for
 
Skin-Goddess
 
Aesthetic
 
Center
 
that
 
will
 
automate
 
appointment
 
scheduling,
 
inventory
 
monitoring,
 
and
 
report

to
 
improve
 
the
 
efficiency
 
and
 
accuracy
 
of
 
the
 
clinic’s
 
operations.
 
Specific  Objectives  
●  To  create  a  web-based  system  that  allows  clients  to  schedule  appointments  online,  
where
 
customers
 
can
 
select
 
their
 
preferred
 
date
 
and
 
time
 
slot.
 ●  To  include  a  feature  that  allows  users  to  pay  reservation  fees  using  the  system.   ●  To  implement  a  feature  that  enables  staff  to  manage  and  monitor  scheduled  
appointments
 
efficiently.
 ●  To  develop  a  notification  feature  that  reminds  users  of  particular  events  or  
appointments.
 ●  To  develop  a  feature  that  monitors  product  stock  levels  in  real  time,  allowing  staff  
to
 
add,
 
delete,
 
and

stock
 
information
 
while
 
tracking
 
available
 
quantities

 ●  To  integrate  a  feature  that  automatically  deducts  inventory  based  on  the  service

 ●  To  incorporate  a  feature  that  allows  users  to  add  products  in  batches  using  OCR.  ●  To  provide  a  feature  that  allows  staff  to  manage  and  monitor  customer  accounts  
and

 ●  To  implement  a  feature  that  enables  staff  to  export  formatted  spreadsheets.

Scope

 ●  A  keyword-based  FAQ  chatbot  that  provides  automated  responses  to  common

Limitations

hosted
 
on

cloud-based
 
platform.
 ●  PayMongo  only  handles  reservation/down  payments,  not  full-service  payments.

 ●  The  accuracy  and  reliability  of  inventory  and  appointment  records  are  dependent

data

 ●  The  chatbot  is  limited  to  predefined  and  keyword-based  responses  and  does  not

artificial

for
 
medical
 
diagnosis
 
or
 
advanced
 
conversational
 
processing
 
 ●  The  system  is  dependent  on  PayMongo  for  reservation  payment  processing;

 ●  The  system  does  not  include  online  consultation  or  any  medical  diagnosis

 ●  The  notifications  are  displayed  only  on  the  system  and  the  user’s  registered  email.  ●  The  system  is  designed  for  a  single  branch  of  Skin-Goddess  Aesthetic  Center  and

 ●  The  system  is  web-based  only  and  does  not  have  a  dedicated  mobile  application.  ●  The  system  does  not  integrate  with  external  POS,  accounting,  or
