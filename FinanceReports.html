Financial record exports

Income exports include posted payments only. All exports use the date range; direction, classification, method, source, and status filters apply to payment and expense records, while cash-closing exports use dates only. Refunds and reversals remain tied to their original payment records. Expense records store dates only; their time and payment method are not recorded.
